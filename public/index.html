What to expect during your Spravato session
Curated clinical team · Updated Oct 2026

Your session is a calm, supervised space. You'll be in a private room with a comfortable recliner, and a member of our team will check in on you regularly.

Before your dose

We'll check your blood pressure when you arrive. Try to avoid eating for 2 hours and drinking liquids for 30 minutes beforehand — this can help with nausea.

During the first hour

Many people notice feeling detached, dreamy, or floaty. Colors and sounds may feel different. This is expected and usually eases within the first hour. Let the music carry you, and remember: you can always tap Alert staff.

"Let it come, let it be, let it go." — a simple phrase many patients find grounding.
Observation & going home

You'll stay with us for about 2 hours. We'll check your blood pressure again before you leave. Please don't drive until the next day after a restful night's sleep — plan a ride or a companion.

After your session

Rest, hydrate, and be gentle with yourself. Jot down any thoughts in your integration journal while they're fresh.

What it is
A prescription nasal spray related to ketamine. You take it yourself in our clinic under staff supervision — it’s never sent home. It’s only available through certified clinics in an FDA safety program (REMS).
Who it may help
FDA-approved for adults with treatment-resistant depression — depression that hasn’t improved enough after trying at least two antidepressants — on its own or with an oral antidepressant. Also approved, with an antidepressant, for adults with major depression who have recent suicidal thoughts or actions (it is not a replacement for emergency care). Not approved for anyone under 18.
What a session is like
We check your blood pressure, then you take the spray and rest in a private, calm room with music or reading. You may feel dreamy, detached, dizzy, sleepy or a little nauseous — this usually eases during the visit. Staff monitor you for at least 2 hours. Plan a ride home: no driving until the next day, after a restful sleep.
How long a course lasts
Usually twice a week for weeks 1–4, then weekly for weeks 5–8. After that, weekly or every other week, as you and your provider decide. Your provider checks how it’s working at the end of week 4.
Insurance & cost
Many plans cover Spravato, usually with prior authorization. Curated accepts Cigna, Aetna and UnitedHealthcare for Spravato, and our team checks your benefits for you. A manufacturer savings program may lower costs for some patients with commercial insurance.
This is general education, not medical advice. Whether a treatment is right for you depends on your health history — talk with your Curated provider.

What it is
A non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic pulses — delivered through a cushioned helmet — to stimulate areas of the brain involved in mood. No anesthesia, no medication and no recovery time.
Who it may help
FDA-cleared for adults with major depression who haven’t improved enough with antidepressant medication in their current episode, including the anxiety that often comes with depression. Also FDA-cleared as an add-on treatment for OCD.
What a session is like
You sit in a comfortable chair with earplugs in while the helmet delivers pulses that feel like tapping on your scalp. You stay awake the whole time, then drive yourself home or back to work. Headache or scalp discomfort are the most common side effects and usually fade; seizures are rare.
How long a course lasts
Typically 5 days a week for 4 weeks, then twice a week for several more weeks. Many people start to notice changes around weeks 3–4, though everyone responds differently.
Insurance & cost
Most major plans, including Medicare, may cover TMS for depression when certain criteria are met (such as prior medication trials), with prior authorization. Curated accepts Cigna, Aetna and UnitedHealthcare for TMS.
This is general education, not medical advice. Whether a treatment is right for you depends on your health history — talk with your Curated provider.
